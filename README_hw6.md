# Homework 6 — Question Formalizations

## Task 1: Noise Complaints

1. **Version 1 — Original Stakeholder Question**

   The mayor wants to know if noise issues tend to stem from different causes across the year.

2. **Version 2 — First Refinement**

   How do the types of noise complaints reported to NYC 311 change throughout 2024?

3. **Version 3 — Second Refinement**

   How does the monthly distribution of NYC 311 noise complaint descriptors change from January through December 2024?

4. **Version 4 — Final Measurable Question**

   For each month in 2024, what are the ten most frequently reported NYC 311 noise complaint descriptors, and what percentage of that month's total noise complaints does each descriptor represent?

## Task 2: Urban Rodents


1. **Version 1 — Original Stakeholder Question**

   Where in New York City are rats and mice most likely to create sanitation issues?

2. **Version 2 — First Refinement**

   What types of buildings, properties, or structures in New York City receive the most complaints about rats and mice?

3. **Version 3 — Second Refinement**

   How does the number of NYC 311 rodent complaints vary across different types of buildings, properties, or structures in 2024?

4. **Version 4 — Final Measurable Question**

   For NYC 311 rodent complaints reported in 2024, how many complaints are associated with each location type, and what percentage of all rodent complaints does each location type represent?
