# NYC 311 Complaint Type Analysis — 2024

## 1. Most Abundant Complaint Type

Using NYC 311 service request records created between January 1 and February 29, 2024, HEAT/HOT WATER was identified as the most abundant complaint type, with 81,631 complaints across the five NYC boroughs.

The Bronx had the highest number of HEAT/HOT WATER complaints (30,139), followed by Brooklyn (20,915), Manhattan (17,604), Queens (12,107), and Staten Island (866).

## 2. Comparison Between January–February and June–July

The number of HEAT/HOT WATER complaints decreased substantially during June–July 2024.

| Borough | Jan–Feb | Jun–Jul |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bronx | 30,139 | 2,227 |
| Brooklyn | 20,915 | 1,929 |
| Manhattan | 17,604 | 1,777 |
| Queens | 12,107 | 835 |
| Staten Island | 866 | 121 |
| **Total** | **81,631** | **6,889** |

The overall number decreased by 74,742 complaints, representing a 91.56% reduction relative to January–February.

All five boroughs experienced decreases.

## 3. Interpretation

The substantial reduction in HEAT/HOT WATER complaints is consistent with seasonal differences in heating demand. During January and February, colder temperatures increase reliance on residential heating systems. During June and July, heating demand is much lower, which may explain the decrease in reported heating-related problems.

However, this analysis is descriptive and does not establish that seasonal temperature differences caused the decrease. The HEAT/HOT WATER category also combines heating and hot-water issues, which may have different seasonal patterns.

The analysis uses records from the preprocessed dataset, which excludes complaints with missing incident ZIP codes.
